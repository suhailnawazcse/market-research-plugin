# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of background, experience, identity, or career stage. We agree to take part respectfully and in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Credit sources and collaborators.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on any protected characteristic.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Requests or content meant to help collect personal data or to target individuals.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and to public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

To report a concern, open a private security advisory on GitHub or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that break this code, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. Decisions are made fairly and explained where possible.
